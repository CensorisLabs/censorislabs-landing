# CensorisLabs launch and marketing plan

## Positioning

Lead with a simple promise: **small, useful tools that keep personal data on your device**. Present CensorisLabs as one family with a consistent design and a free, donation-supported model, while each app has its own clear job. Avoid claims such as “all sources,” “instant news,” guaranteed financial results, or complete streaming availability.

## Who each app helps

| Audience | Lead app | First demo |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Indian households | ArthaSetu | Add a few transactions, see an Indian-format budget, and export a local backup |
| Everyday health planners | Calorie Calculator | Compare maintenance and goal estimates; make the non-medical scope clear |
| PC builders | PC Part Picker | Check a sample build and show where compatibility estimates can be incomplete |
| Indian finance readers | Indian Banking Brief | Open RBI/SEBI source links and show the refresh timestamp |
| Renters and homeowners | Home Energy Estimator | Enter appliance use and a local tariff to estimate a bill |
| Muslim households | Nur Companion | Read a credited Qur’an translation and verify prayer times against the local mosque |
| Federated social users | Open Social | Browse a public Mastodon-compatible timeline and explain the session-only token |
| Students and researchers | Archive Explorer | Find a public catalog item and inspect its rights information |
| Casual players | Offline Arcade | Start a game in airplane mode; keep the demo under one minute |
| Film and TV fans | Where to Watch | Search a title and show country-specific provider links; state licensing/data limits |
| Readers of world affairs | World Conflict Brief | Compare original publisher links and the last-indexed time |

## Launch sequence

1. Publish the apps pages first, with clear free features, supported platforms, source notes, and live download status.
2. Recruit a small opt-in beta from the audiences above. Ask testers to complete one core task on both a lower-end Android phone and a Windows PC.
3. Fix onboarding friction and package-size issues before a public release. Publish a versioned release only after its Android signatures and install files are verified.
4. Keep every feature free. Invite optional donations through the public Donate page; never gate a task or show a checkout.
5. Start with two app-specific stories a week: a short screen recording, a source-backed tip, or a before/after workflow. Reuse the recording as a blog post and a vertical video.

## Low-cost channels

- Write search pages around specific intent: Indian expense tracking offline, appliance electricity estimate, public archive search, and offline Android games. Link each article to its matching app page.
- Share demos in relevant communities only where self-promotion is allowed. Ask for feedback before asking for a download.
- Publish changelogs and signed release notes with every tag. Pin one practical app demo and one privacy explanation on the CensorisLabs social accounts.
- Offer a downloadable one-page guide per app (budget worksheet, PC build checklist, energy estimate worksheet, or research/source checklist) that remains useful without installing anything.
- Ask early users for permission before quoting feedback; never publish finance, health, location, or religious activity data.

## Trust and retention

- Put source links and timestamps beside news headlines. For developing conflict reports, encourage readers to compare publishers and distinguish index time from event time.
- Keep the Qur’an reader's translation credits visible and verify prayer time method/location settings with local users.
- Say plainly that calorie and tax calculators are estimates, and do not frame them as medical, tax, or investment advice.
- Don't promise instant closed-app alerts without a notification service. Don't describe a provider list as exhaustive.
- Offer a clear export/delete path for locally saved data and explain which actions contact third-party sources.

## Measure the launch

Track only aggregate counts that users knowingly share: app-page visits, installer clicks, successful first opens, first completed task, 7-day return, crash reports, and source freshness errors. Avoid invasive ad tracking. Compare by app and platform so a weak install flow is not confused with weak product demand.

## Offers

Use one consistent message across the suite: every app and every feature is free. Donations are optional, one-off, and handled by the user’s own UPI app; there are no coupons, tiers, trials, or checkout flows.
