AbsoluteCal Health Connect privacy

AbsoluteCal is a free, personal health and calorie tracker published by CensorisLabs. Health Connect access is optional and is requested only after you choose to connect or sync.

Data access and use

With your permission, AbsoluteCal reads the specific Health Connect data types shown in Android’s permission screen, such as steps, distance, active calories, heart rate, sleep, selected vital signs, weight and body fat. The app uses those values to populate your local tracker and display your own history.

AbsoluteCal does not sell Health Connect data, use it for advertising, or send it to CensorisLabs servers. Imported data is stored in the app’s local storage on your device. You can remove it in the app or revoke access in Health Connect settings. Revoke access at any time.

Other optional network requests

If you look up a packaged food by barcode, the barcode is sent to Open Food Facts. Lab report and meal photos are processed on your device; report images are not uploaded. The OCR engine may download language files when first used.
