Pick your device, then set up your money in about five minutes.

Your first five minutes

No account is needed. Everything below happens on your device.

  1. Pick your language

    Choose English or one of seven Indian languages. You can change it later in settings.

  2. Set your starting balances

    Enter the cash you hold and your bank balances, so the dashboard starts correct.

  3. Add your first expense

    Use quick-add for chai, auto or groceries. Amounts appear in lakhs and crores.

  4. Add your regular bills

    Enter rent, electricity, mobile recharge and EMIs so reminders can start.

  5. Protect your data

    Export an encrypted copy of your records, or use optional encrypted backup across devices.

On Android you can also turn on SMS import. It is optional and processed on your device.
